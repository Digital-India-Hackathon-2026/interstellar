## Idea
SafeStreet AI is an AI-powered women's safety platform that proactively detects distress situations without requiring users to press an SOS button. By intelligently analyzing real-time smartphone signals such as voice, movement, location patterns, and contextual data, the system identifies potential threats and instantly shares live location and emergency alerts with trusted contacts and authorities. Unlike traditional safety apps that rely on manual activation, SafeStreet AI acts automatically during critical situations when victims may be unable to seek help. The platform also generates anonymous safety heatmaps to identify high-risk areas, helping citizens make safer travel decisions while supporting authorities in improving public safety infrastructure. By combining artificial intelligence, real-time emergency response, and community-driven safety insights, SafeStreet AI aims to create a safer, smarter, and more secure India for women.
